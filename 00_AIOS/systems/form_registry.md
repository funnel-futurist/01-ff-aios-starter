# Form registry

Use this registry for your company's own forms. Check it before building a new form so each purpose has one clear home. Start with empty rows and fill them from your own records; a blank field means unknown, not confirmed absent.

## Your forms

## Status and title rules

| Registry status | Meaning | Form title |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Live | Retained for use as it is. | Plain title, with no status bracket. |
| [MAIN] | The target version that receives a merge. | Plain title; MAIN is a registry role, not a title prefix. |
| [MERGE] | Useful questions move into the named main form. Retirement follows accepted merge evidence. | Prefix with `[MERGE]`. |
| [ARCHIVE] | Retained for its history or required responses, outside current use. | Prefix with `[ARCHIVE]`; the archive workspace is `99-archive`. |
| [DELETE] | Proposed for deletion under an approved retention and action decision. | Prefix with `[DELETE]`; the label alone grants no deletion authority. |
| Unknown | The status or its supporting evidence has not been established. | Preserve the existing title until resolved. |

A main becomes Live after its merge is accepted. Record the source and target explicitly; do not mark every version MERGE and leave the survivor unclear. These rules describe the intended organization. A registry entry does not prove a title change, move, merge or deletion occurred.

## Source of truth and checks

For each row, link the form's native configuration or other authoritative record you can access in this company's workspace. Include the date checked. Record an approved decision separately when it describes a future change, so intent is not mistaken for observed behavior.

Verify the notification destination from the form's connection or routing evidence. Record the channel or other destination and its evidence, or mark it unknown. Use “none” only after checking that no connection exists; a missing entry is not proof. An account connection does not by itself prove delivery.

Before a merge, identify the exact forms, preserve required responses and compare their questions. Accept the target and check its consumers before retiring the source. Before an archive, move or deletion, follow the owner's authorized scope and retention decision and record the resulting readback. Preserve unresolved duplicates and holds rather than guessing which record survives.

Update this registry when a form's purpose, owner, workspace, status or routing changes. Keep planned work and unresolved questions in the relevant row's source-of-truth record until there is evidence to close them.
