## Choose the starting pathway

| Pathway | Starting situation | First practical objective |
|---|---|---|
| DIY Builder | A founder or operator is building and operating the system | Complete one bounded job and explain the review and acceptance steps |
| First Operator | A business is adding its first person to run the system | Transfer one real outcome with clear ownership and working context |
| Existing Team | People already work in the business | Map current responsibilities, correct gaps and prove the next handoff |
| Certified Placement | An operator joins through a placement or certification route | Verify the person's actual readiness for the assigned role and first live task |

These are ways to adopt the operating method, not product tiers or proof of certification. Use the actual hiring and certification records.

## Development stages

| Stage | Required input | Practice or action | Evidence to move forward |
|---|---|---|---|
| Define role and outcome | Business constraint, required result and accountable manager | Define the responsibilities, working conditions and evidence needed from the role | A clear role brief and acceptance standard; use an existing role where suitable |
| Source and select | Approved role brief and hiring process | Source candidates where needed and assess the actual skills through relevant work | Selection rationale and assessment evidence; an existing teammate can record why sourcing is not needed |
| Agreement and scoped access | Selected person, approved agreement and access owner | Complete the appropriate engagement agreement and grant only the access needed | Agreement and correct authorized access verified; authority and escalation route understood |
| Orientation | Company foundations and current priorities | Read the relevant context and explain the customer journey in the person's own words | Accurate explanation of the next owned handoff |
| Method and tools | One bounded task and its quality standard | Find the correct brain, read a verified method and locate company inputs | Correct method revision, input sources and destination |
| Supervised first result | Agreed task and available reviewer | Complete a branch or draft, check it and present the change | Reviewed result with evidence and specific corrections resolved |
| Independent work | Accepted practice result and clear authority | Repeat the workflow using the normal review process | Useful result accepted in real use; exceptions handled correctly |
| Continued development | Outcome evidence and observed gaps | Improve judgment, consistency and scope through the role's 30/60/90 plan | The manager can point to stronger results, not just completed lessons |
| Progression or role change | Sustained evidence, business need and an owner decision | Agree revised responsibilities, compensation where applicable and access changes through their actual approval routes | New role expectations accepted and handoffs verified; no automatic promotion |
| Offboarding and continuity | Confirmed end or transfer of responsibility | Hand over work, document outstanding items and execute authorized access changes | Receiving owner can continue; revocation and records-retention evidence recorded |

## The PR workflow, explained from the ground up

A **branch** is a separate proposed version of the company's files. A **pull request (PR)** shows the change so it can be checked before it enters the shared version. A **commit** identifies the exact revision being reviewed.

Before asking a person to approve, explain the business problem, the change, the evidence and the consequence. Use the existing `explain-from-the-ground-up` skill when any part is unclear.

- Passing automated checks means those checks ran and passed.
- An independent technical review evaluates the proposed change.
- An eligible human approval supplies any required owner decision.
- Merging integrates the source; it does not prove a deployment or business result.
- Acceptance in use comes from the actual workflow and the person who owns the outcome.

Approval may be given through the configured interface or an authorized terminal command. A terminal command does not bypass required review or permissions. Re-read the PR's exact current head and show the consequential action before executing the already-authorized step.
