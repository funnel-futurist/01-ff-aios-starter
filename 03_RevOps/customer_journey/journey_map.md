## Stage map

The named brains supply reusable methods. The company's people and configured systems perform the work. Replace examples with the company's actual implementation before treating a stage as ready.

| Stage | Entry and required inputs | Lead method and contributing brains | Company home and handoff | Exit evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Attention and authority | Approved audience, offer, claims and channel plan | Attention; Copy for messages, Creation for assets | `04_Attention/` → configured capture destination | Published asset verified where authorized; measurable CTA destination |
| Capture | Person responds or submits consented information | Profit; Copy for form/page language | `03_RevOps/funnels/` and `crm/` | Test or real lead reaches correct record with consent, source and owner |
| Conversion and nurture | Captured lead with an allowed contact route | Profit; Copy for follow-up | `03_RevOps/automations/` and `sales/` | Qualification and next step recorded; authorized messages delivered or failure owned |
| Close | Qualified opportunity and approved offer | Profit; Copy for sales material | `03_RevOps/sales/` | Explicit commercial decision, accurate scope and agreed next action; a booked call alone is not a win |
| Contract and payment | Accepted commercial proposal | Profit; approved legal and financial sources | `03_RevOps/records/` references signed documents and payment system | Required agreement executed, payment condition satisfied and exceptions resolved |
| Onboarding | Confirmed purchased scope and delivery owner | Team; Profit for customer handoff | `02_Team_Ops/onboarding/` and this journey | Account/access prerequisites, context, roles, support route and kickoff are verified |
| Activation | Minimum context and access available | Team coordinates; relevant brains serve the diagnosed constraint | [90-day roadmap](activation_roadmap_90_days.md) linked to the existing project | First useful result accepted; baseline, milestone owners and next actions agreed |
| Delivery and improvement | Active roadmap and measurable baseline | Brain selected by the constraint and task | Existing `02_Team_Ops/projects/` plan, with outputs in owning domains | Milestones accepted using evidence; blockers and plan changes recorded |
| Retention, expansion or offboarding | Review date, outcome evidence or customer decision | Profit and Team; Attention only for separately authorized proof/referrals | `03_RevOps/records/` and existing project | Agreed continuation or documented exit; access and handover executed under actual terms |

The sequence describes the normal path. Record an explicit exception rather than forcing every offer through the same steps. An existing customer can enter at a later stage; a payment failure or missing prerequisite can hold one handoff without blocking unrelated work.

## The two journeys meet in activation

A customer needs an outcome and a roadmap. The person delivering it needs the role, authority and skill to complete the next task. Link the [operator journey](../../02_Team_Ops/onboarding/operator_journey.md) to the actual stage output that person owns.

The [90-day roadmap](activation_roadmap_90_days.md) turns the diagnosed constraint into 30/60/90-day outcomes. The [engagement timeline](engagement_timeline.md) records dates and agreed touchpoints. Keep signed scope and commercial terms in their existing authoritative record.
