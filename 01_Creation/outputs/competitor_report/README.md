Owner: the existing offer/research owner. Copy owns the argument; Creation owns document production. The receiver is the named authorized client reviewer in the existing job. A missing destination or reader identity is a hold, never permission to create or share a substitute.

## Ordered outputs

1. **Evidence-backed comparison:** produce dated findings with source links, what was actually observed, unknowns and the customer implication. Use the approved competitor method named by the company router. No opt-in, application, booking, paid scraping or private competitor access follows from this route.
2. **Report copy:** turn those findings into a supported decision, trade-off and next test. Keep source evidence and inference distinct. Follow the [existing owned Copy production sequence](https://github.com/funnel-futurist/12-ff-copy-brain/blob/25bd04f2347fe37245109c56d0bd222748782add/00_System/sop_copy_production_workflow_v1.md#step-2--pre-draft-excellence-load-the-airlock): identify the mode, complete its pre-draft load, write and score the copy, then run its ordered QC stack before handoff. This is the maintained gate, not a second report-writing method. Keep its required company truth, voice and claim sources; missing access or inputs holds drafting. Do not invent results or copy competitor language.
3. **Private document and receiving receipt:** use the company's authorized document-production adapter. Preserve the report source, document revision, readable headings, material-claim links and reviewer result. Open the exact native Google Doc under the intended authorized receiver before claiming delivery. A local Markdown, HTML, PDF or DOCX is a draft artifact, not that receipt.

The [pinned Creation level gate](https://github.com/funnel-futurist/ff-creation-system/blob/1a61a09ab8b383de17236072a33450d738d0c97c/sources/installation/.claude/skills/document-production-router/SKILL.md#level-gates) requires Level 1 writing approval before Level 2 visual production. This is source evidence at FF's configured internal release, not permission for a generic Starter or a new release pin. ## Inputs and dependencies

Read approved company audience, offer, proof and writing defaults from their existing authoritative home. Job inputs are the decision being made, named peer set, permitted surfaces and evidence window, client-specific facts, budget/effect limits, exact private destination and intended reviewer. Ask once for only missing job inputs. Store them with the job; do not duplicate the company's facts or upload them into Evergreen.

Follow any shared method only at the configured, entitled exact revision. If the method, company defaults, document adapter or native receiver is unavailable, name the exact missing path or permission and stop that step. Earlier authorized research or fictional local preparation can continue. This output adds no writer or runtime.
